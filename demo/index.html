Nested glass

The surface below is the same .ins-glass class, de-nested automatically.

No border, no shadow, no sheen, no second blur — by virtue of being nested at all.

Inner surface

.ins-glass-inner — the de-nested look without the nesting.

The nested card is the discipline that keeps this design language from turning to mush: a second frost over an already frosted ground reads as fog. It is a rule at zero specificity, not a class to remember, so nesting alone triggers it.

One brand variable · متغيّر واحد

Each swatch calls Insiyab.brand(hex) with one value. The triplet, the gradient end-stop, the readable shade and both page washes are derived from it — and the label colour is measured, not assumed, which is why the pale gold takes near-black while the rest take white.
